# AEONFALL — Factions, Hiring, and Taming

## Faction Principles
Factions are gameplay systems, not just lore labels.

Each faction owns:
- territory preferences
- hostility matrix
- reputation progression
- hireable roster
- shop inventory
- crafting specialties
- world-event responses
- enemy priorities
- travel permissions
- unique visual language

## Initial Factions

### The Rift Wardens
Defensive dimensional military order.
- rescue/interception
- portal control
- anti-anomaly contracts

### The Wild Court
Predatory druidic monarchy.
- taming
- apex wildlife
- monster tracking
- living armor

### The Sepulchral Concord
Necromancers who regulate the use of remains rather than treating all death as chaos.
- corpse economy
- bone construction
- spectral echoes
- morally ambiguous contracts

### The Ashen Synod
Infernal rebels who weaponize fire without serving demon courts.
- hazard conversion
- forge access
- demon hunting
- heat armor

### The Cartographers of Elsewhere
Explorers mapping spatially impossible routes.
- secret discovery
- portal anchors
- exotic traversal
- anomaly markets

### The Storm Throne
Skyborne warrior houses.
- aerial combat
- storm domains
- vehicle/flight access
- weather events

### The Resonance Choir
Weaponized sound and rhythm order.
- sonic support
- resonance weapons
- anti-silence technology
- harmonic puzzles

### The Iron Oracles
Machine seers predicting attack patterns from battlefield telemetry.
- defensive prediction
- machine crafting
- vehicle systems
- construct warfare

### The Solar Remnant
Survivors and zealots of Helios Sepulcher.
- solar weapons
- shield technology
- daylight trials
- heliostat control

### The Null Apostates
Defectors from anti-ability void cults.
- denial mechanics
- doctrine knowledge
- dangerous Absolute research

## Reputation
Recommended reputation layers:
1. Unknown
2. Tolerated
3. Contracted
4. Trusted
5. Blood-Sworn
6. Exalted

Reputation can unlock:
- hire slots
- new contracts
- faction recipes
- special travel
- faction-specific class trials
- faction raids

## Hiring
Hireables should have:
- hiring cost/access condition
- active combat identity
- one signature ability
- command vocabulary
- downed/retreat rules
- dismissal behavior
- faction reactions
- performance budget

High-tier hireables must never become passive stat sticks.

## Aggression → Taming
Eligible aggressive creatures can move through states:
Hostile → Observed → Challenged → Yielding → Bonded

Taming methods can differ:
- feed
- exhaust
- survive
- remove parasite
- defend nest
- perform species-specific gesture/timing challenge

Bosses and protected mythic creatures are excluded unless explicitly authored as special quest outcomes.

## Faction Conflict
Regional events may change:
- patrol composition
- shops
- temporary safe routes
- local monster pressure
- hireable availability
- encounter rewards

Persistent player reputation should not require the entire world territory state to persist globally between unrelated sessions.
