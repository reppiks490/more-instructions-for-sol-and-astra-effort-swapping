# AEONFALL — Horde Evolution and Horror Ecosystem

## Goal
The horde is not a generic zombie reskin.

It is a predatory ecology whose members compete, consume one another, inherit traits, and can be manipulated by necromancers, demons, or eldritch forces.

## Five Cannibal Evolution Tiers

### Tier I — The Famished
Fast, desperate pack creatures.
- climb and vault aggressively
- attracted to wounded creatures
- compete for corpses
- weak individually
- high pack readability

### Tier II — The Gorged
Individuals that successfully consume enough biomass.
- visibly heavier anatomy
- temporary armor/fat reserves
- can body-block routes
- release hunger calls that redirect nearby Famished

### Tier III — The Remade
Mutation depends on what they consumed and how nearby brood members died.
Branches include:
- plated
- sprinter
- spitter
- burrower
- screamer
- corpse-carrier

Only one or two traits should dominate any single entity to preserve readability.

### Tier IV — The Apostate
Rare intelligent cannibals that deliberately farm lesser brood members.
- command packs
- set ambushes around feeding sites
- choose mutation timing
- may bargain with necromancers
- can interrupt player revive/rescue patterns

### Tier V — The Crowned Hunger
Regional apexes created when a horde reaches a critical biomass/event threshold.
- multi-phase elite/boss behavior
- dynamically consumes lesser brood during combat
- chooses one counter-mutation based on observed player tactics
- can seed future outbreaks if allowed to escape

## Corpse Economy
Corpses are a strategic resource shared by:
- cannibal hordes
- necromancers
- scavenger wildlife
- selected crafting objectives
- cleansing objectives

Players can:
- burn remains
- sanctify remains
- harvest remains
- bait predators with remains
- deny necromancers resources

This turns defeated enemies into continued encounter decisions.

## Necromancer Archetypes
- **Ossuary Engineer:** constructs battlefield infrastructure.
- **Grave Cantor:** turns nearby deaths into buff/debuff rhythms.
- **Marrow Taxer:** marks living targets and collects power when marked entities fall.
- **Sepulchral Midwife:** evolves corpses into specialized undead rather than basic resurrection.
- **Black Archivist:** records defeated archetypes and summons imperfect echoes.
- **King Without Pulse:** high-tier necromancer commander controlling multiple corpse economies.

## Demon Families
Demons are not interchangeable with undead.
- ritual demons
- execution demons
- possession entities
- contract demons
- siege demons
- infernal beasts
- court demons

Demon encounters emphasize bargains, rituals, domains, marks, and rule-bound powers.

## Eldritch Families
Eldritch entities should feel causally wrong rather than simply grotesque.
- observation predators
- classification entities
- geometry parasites
- false landmarks
- memory eaters
- impossible weather organisms
- dimensional taxonomists
- cosmic larvae

## Horror Readability Rule
Scary design still requires fair combat:
- signature attacks telegraph
- mutations remain visually consistent
- threat priority is readable
- full-screen distortion is limited
- audio cues have accessibility substitutes
- no unavoidable instant-kill horror gimmicks
