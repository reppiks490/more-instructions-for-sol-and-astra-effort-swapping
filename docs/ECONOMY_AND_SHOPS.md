# AEONFALL — Economy, Shops, and Crafting

## Economy Layers

### 1. Combat Economy
Ammo-like resources, temporary consumables, encounter pickups, short-lived buffs.

### 2. Crafting Economy
Persistent or session-tracked materials used for:
- weapons
- armor
- relics
- upgrades
- refinement
- boss keys
- Axiom Heart progression

### 3. Reputation Economy
Faction standing unlocks:
- hireables
- recipes
- traversal permissions
- faction shops
- cosmetic/lore rewards
- special contracts

### 4. Premium Entitlements
Where Epic's current in-island transaction system permits, paid content must use official Fortnite/Verse marketplace entitlement systems and comply with Epic policy.

No hidden payment path, no external payment workaround, and no unauthorized creator-only commerce bypass.

Developer/creator testing privileges should be implemented as clearly identified development/test configuration, not as a concealed paid-item bypass in production.

## Shop Types
- wandering merchant
- faction quartermaster
- black-market anomaly shop
- boss trophy exchange
- armor forge
- weapon forge
- vehicle foundry
- beast-taming lodge
- relic archivist
- world-event emergency vendor
- endgame Axiom Forge

## Shop Inventory Logic
Inventories can rotate by:
- region state
- faction control
- player reputation
- boss kills
- current world event
- class mastery
- season/content version
- hidden discovery flags

## Unique Item Design
High-tier shop items require:
- nontrivial mechanic
- explicit counterplay
- visual transformation
- acquisition story
- progression connection

Do not fill shops with stat-only duplicates.

## Axiom Heart Crafting
The final craft represents tens of thousands of acquired inputs through nested refinement.

Example pipeline:
raw drop → refined family material → regional catalyst → boss-bound component → Absolute relic → Axiom subassembly → Axiom Heart.

The ledger stores quantities by canonical material ID so thousands of resources do not require thousands of physical spawned objects.

## Power and Monetization
Core competitive legitimacy rule:
- Transcendent Zero cannot be directly purchased.
- A paid entitlement cannot skip the Choosing or mandatory mastery/trial gates.
- Paid content may grant authorized access/content where permitted, but the pinnacle progression remains earned.

This preserves the meaning of the chosen-few system.
