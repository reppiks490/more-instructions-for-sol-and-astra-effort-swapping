# AEONFALL — World Regions

The world must feel like a single impossible continent with strongly differentiated ecological and combat identities. Names should feel mythic, memorable, and specific rather than generic biome labels.

## 1. The Crownless March
A vast moving-shadow plain beneath the route of the Crownless Continent.
- broken fortresses
- migratory monster fronts
- moving colossus landmarks
- siege faction conflict
- God-tier event staging

## 2. Vesperglass Dominion
A city-region built from black reflective towers and fractured transit canyons.
- mirror predators
- impossible sightlines
- vertical traversal
- hidden reflective routes
- assassination contracts

## 3. The Ossuary Meridian
A continental necropolis crossed by bone aqueducts and grave-engine roads.
- necromancer city-states
- corpse economy
- siege ossuaries
- underground archive vaults
- undead civil wars

## 4. Helios Sepulcher
A dead solar empire where enormous heliostat ruins still track the sky.
- solar weapon crafting
- heat hazards
- sun cults
- buried observatories
- daylight-dependent encounters

## 5. Blacktide Expanse
A drowned coastline of abyssal trenches, storm reefs, and half-submerged megastructures.
- leviathans
- amphibious traversal
- tide events
- drowned ruins
- moving ocean bosses

## 6. The Maw Between Stars
A void-corrupted highland where geography intermittently misaligns.
- eldritch anomalies
- false landmarks
- spatial shortcuts
- observation predators
- Absolute-tier archive chain

## 7. Ember Throne Caldera
A volcanic kingdom built into layered crater cities.
- infernal factions
- demon tribunals
- living forges
- ash wildlife
- fire-domain boss arenas

## 8. The Pale Resonance
An enormous frozen plateau filled with resonant ice formations that react to combat.
- sonic hazards
- ice fortresses
- buried machines
- avalanche events
- White Reaver territory

## 9. Worldroot Cathedra
A forest of colossal living architecture where roots form bridges, towers, and underground sanctuaries.
- Beast-Crown factions
- tameable apex wildlife
- living dungeons
- primordial crafting
- Worldroot class trials

## 10. The Chronarch Scar
A desert of failed time experiments where ruins exist in several authored states.
- replay encounters
- temporal puzzles
- phase-shifted POIs
- Chronarch mastery
- state-recording boss mechanics

## 11. Null Basilica
A silent black-white metropolis whose laws change by district.
- rule-domain trials
- anti-ability hunters
- doctrine shrines
- Absolute progression
- low-color high-contrast visual identity

## 12. The Zero Reliquary
Hidden endgame region assembled from fragments of every other region.
- Zero Candidate-only routes
- Axiom Forge
- Ascension Trial staging
- Choosing arena
- Hall of Ascendants

## Region Design Contract
Every region requires:
- 3+ major POIs
- 2+ dungeons/strongholds
- 1 faction hub
- 1 roaming threat
- 1 regional boss
- 1 event chain
- 1 traversal identity
- material families
- wildlife ecology
- streaming budget
- event/boss reserve
- distant landmark silhouette
